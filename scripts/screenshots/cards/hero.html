Herdr mod · macOS · SSH

Filer Pro

The file panel for Herdr, on your Mac and on every machine you reach over SSH.
  • Follows your terminal, local or remote
  • Two trees: a machine above, This Mac below
  • Upload, download, copy and move between them
  • Select several files and act on all
  • Preview Markdown, PDF, HTML and images
  • Edit in place or in your editor
